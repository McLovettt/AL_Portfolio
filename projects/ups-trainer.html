Adam’s UPS Trainer v0.7

A browser-based UPS switching simulator created to make power paths and switching states easier to explore during training.

← Back to all projects

The project

The trainer models multiple UPS-related scenarios through interactive controls. It includes single UPS and parallel UPS views, a Castell-key section and a lights-and-auxiliaries section. Visual states are intended to help explain which paths are available, energised or in transition.

Engineering focus

The core of the project is applying real UPS systems knowledge to training-oriented logic: supply availability, bypass paths, battery conditions, indications and consequences of incorrect switching sequences.

What it demonstrates

Translating a complex electrical system into a user interface, structuring front-end logic, validating changing conditions and designing around safety-sensitive scenarios.

Important limitation

This is a learning and demonstration tool only. It does not replace manufacturer documentation, authorisation or site-specific switching procedures.
